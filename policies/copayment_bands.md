# Copayment Bands

## Clause CG-COPAY-001: Standard Copayment Table

| Plan | GP Visit | Specialist Visit | Inpatient Admission | Emergency Room |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | AED 50 | AED 100 | AED 500 | AED 200 |
| Enhanced | AED 30 | AED 60 | AED 0 | AED 100 |
| Thiqa | AED 0 | AED 0 | AED 0 | AED 0 |
| Comprehensive | AED 0 | AED 0 | AED 0 | AED 0 |

These copayment values apply at the point of care and are deducted before
the insurer's reimbursement percentage is calculated on the remaining billed
amount.

## Clause CG-COPAY-002: Pharmacy Copayment

Generic medications carry a flat AED 20 copayment across Basic and Enhanced
plans; branded medications where a generic equivalent exists carry a AED 50
copayment plus 20% coinsurance on the price difference. Thiqa and
Comprehensive members are exempt from pharmacy copayments for medications on
the approved formulary (see `pharmacy_benefit_policy.md`).

## Clause CG-COPAY-003: Copayment Waivers for Chronic Disease Management

Members enrolled in an approved chronic disease management program have
their GP and specialist copayments waived for visits directly related to the
management of the enrolled condition, across all plan tiers, as an incentive
for continuity of care.
