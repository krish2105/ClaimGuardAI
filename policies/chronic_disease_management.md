# Chronic Disease Management Policy

## Clause CG-CDM-001: Program Enrollment

Members diagnosed with a qualifying chronic condition (Type 2 diabetes
mellitus E11.x, essential hypertension I10, chronic obstructive pulmonary
disease J44.9, or heart failure I50.9) may enroll in the Chronic Disease
Management (CDM) program. Enrollment unlocks the copayment waivers in
Clause CG-COPAY-003 and the Enhanced-tier consultation uplift in Clause
CG-ENH-005.

## Clause CG-CDM-002: Required Monitoring Cadence

CDM-enrolled diabetic members are covered for HbA1c testing (CPT 83036) up
to 4 times per policy year at 100% reimbursement with no copayment,
reflecting the recommended clinical monitoring cadence. Requests for HbA1c
testing beyond 6 times per year without a documented clinical
justification (e.g., medication titration) should be flagged by the Coding
Agent as a potential over-utilization pattern.

## Clause CG-CDM-003: Medication Adherence Coverage

Chronic maintenance medications for enrolled conditions are covered under
the pharmacy benefit at the CDM-preferred copayment tier (see
`pharmacy_benefit_policy.md`), provided the prescribing physician is the
member's registered CDM care coordinator or a referred specialist.
