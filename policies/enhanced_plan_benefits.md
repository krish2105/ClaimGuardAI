# Enhanced Benefit Plan — Coverage Schedule

*Synthetic mid-tier plan styled after typical UAE private insurer "Enhanced"
or "Silver" product tiers. Illustrative figures only.*

## Clause CG-ENH-001: Inpatient Hospitalization Coverage

The Enhanced Plan covers inpatient hospitalization in Category B, C, and D
network facilities at 100% of the Standard Fee Schedule with no per-admission
copayment. Category A (premium) facilities are covered at 90%. Private room
accommodation is included as standard. Elective admissions require prior
authorization at least 2 business days in advance; emergency admissions must
be notified within 72 hours, a longer grace window than the Basic Plan in
recognition of the Enhanced Plan's broader emergency network.

## Clause CG-ENH-002: Outpatient Consultation Coverage

Outpatient consultations are covered at 90% after a flat AED 30 copayment for
general practitioners and AED 60 for specialists, with up to 24 subsidized
specialist consultations per policy year. Video/telehealth consultations are
reimbursed at the same rate as in-person visits when delivered by a
DHA-licensed practitioner.

## Clause CG-ENH-003: Diagnostic Imaging and Advanced Procedures

Advanced imaging (MRI, CT, PET) is covered at 85% and requires prior
authorization for elective cases only; imaging ordered from within an
emergency department encounter is exempt from prior authorization under this
clause. Cardiac catheterization and percutaneous coronary intervention (CPT
93458, 92928) are covered at 80% subject to prior authorization and a
second-opinion recommendation for elective (non-emergency) cases above AED
20,000 in billed value.

## Clause CG-ENH-004: Annual and Lifetime Limits

The Enhanced Plan carries an annual benefit limit of AED 1,000,000 per member
per policy year, with no aggregate lifetime limit. Sub-limits apply to
maternity (see `maternity_coverage.md`), dental, and optical categories.

## Clause CG-ENH-005: Chronic Disease Support Uplift

Members enrolled in an approved chronic disease management program (Clause
CG-CDM-001) receive an additional 4 subsidized specialist consultations per
year beyond the standard Enhanced allowance, and a waived copayment on
HbA1c and lipid panel laboratory tests.
