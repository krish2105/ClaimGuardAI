# Pharmacy Benefit Policy

## Clause CG-RX-001: Formulary Tiers

The pharmacy benefit uses a three-tier formulary: Tier 1 (generic, lowest
copayment), Tier 2 (preferred branded), and Tier 3 (non-preferred
branded/specialty). Basic and Enhanced members pay the copayment schedule
in Clause CG-COPAY-002; Thiqa and Comprehensive members are exempt from
copayment on Tier 1 and Tier 2 medications.

## Clause CG-RX-002: Prescription Duration Limits

A single pharmacy claim may cover up to a 90-day supply for chronic
maintenance medications and up to a 30-day supply for acute/short-course
medications. Claims for quantities beyond these limits without a documented
travel or clinical exception should be flagged for coding review as a
potential stockpiling or diversion risk.

## Clause CG-RX-003: Specialty and High-Cost Medication Prior Authorization

Specialty medications (biologics, oncology therapies, and any single
medication claim exceeding AED 2,000) require prior authorization
regardless of plan tier, and are subject to the same escalation logic as
CPT-coded specialty procedures under `prior_authorization_requirements.md`.
