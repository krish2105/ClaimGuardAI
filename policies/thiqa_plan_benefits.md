# Thiqa Plan — Coverage Schedule (Abu Dhabi Nationals-Style)

*Synthetic plan styled after the Thiqa program available to UAE nationals in
Abu Dhabi. Illustrative figures only — not a reproduction of any real Thiqa
policy wording.*

## Clause CG-THQ-001: Inpatient Hospitalization Coverage

The Thiqa Plan covers inpatient hospitalization at 100% across all network
facility categories (A through D) with no per-admission copayment for UAE
national members. Non-network facility admissions are covered at 100% of the
Category B equivalent rate. There is no limit on the number of admissions per
policy year for medically necessary care.

## Clause CG-THQ-002: Outpatient and Specialist Coverage

Outpatient consultations, including specialist and sub-specialist visits, are
covered at 100% with no copayment, and there is no annual cap on the number of
subsidized consultations. Prior authorization is still required for
high-cost elective procedures per `prior_authorization_requirements.md`, but
the authorization turnaround SLA for Thiqa members is expedited to 24 hours
per `claims_submission_sla.md`.

## Clause CG-THQ-003: Chronic and Complex Condition Coverage

Thiqa members with chronic conditions (diabetes, cardiovascular disease,
chronic kidney disease, cancer) are covered at 100% for all related
consultations, diagnostics, medications, and procedures, including advanced
therapies such as chemotherapy administration (CPT 96413) and radiotherapy
planning (CPT 77301), without an annual benefit ceiling on these categories.

## Clause CG-THQ-004: Annual Benefit Limit

The Thiqa Plan does not apply a fixed annual monetary limit for medically
necessary treatment. Claims exceeding AED 100,000 in billed value are
automatically routed for a compliance/medical-necessity review regardless of
the fraud score returned by the Fraud Scoring Agent, in line with the
plan's high-value-claim governance requirement.
