# Exclusions and Limitations

## Clause CG-EXC-001: Cosmetic and Elective Non-Medical Procedures

Cosmetic procedures performed for aesthetic purposes rather than
reconstructive or medically necessary reasons are excluded under all plan
tiers, including the Comprehensive Plan. This includes elective cosmetic
dermatology, elective rhinoplasty, and non-reconstructive plastic surgery.
Claims coded with a cosmetic-only CPT/ICD-10 combination should be
recommended for denial with this clause cited, not escalated, unless the
procedure follows a documented trauma or medical condition (in which case it
is reconstructive, not cosmetic, and is evaluated under the relevant
specialty policy instead).

## Clause CG-EXC-002: Pre-Existing Condition Waiting Period

Newly enrolled members are subject to a 6-month waiting period for claims
related to a pre-existing chronic condition disclosed at enrollment, except
for Thiqa members, who have no waiting period under Clause CG-THQ-003.
Claims for a pre-existing condition within the waiting period should be
denied under this clause.

## Clause CG-EXC-003: Self-Inflicted Injury and Substance-Related Claims

Treatment for self-inflicted injury or injury sustained while under the
influence of alcohol or non-prescribed controlled substances is excluded
under all plans, in line with standard UAE market practice. Claims with an
ICD-10 code explicitly indicating self-harm or intoxication as the primary
cause of injury should be denied under this clause.

## Clause CG-EXC-004: Experimental and Unproven Treatments

Treatments not approved by the UAE Ministry of Health and Prevention (MoHAP)
or DHA, or explicitly labeled experimental/investigational by the treating
facility, are excluded from coverage regardless of plan tier.

## Clause CG-EXC-005: Out-of-Network Elective Care

Elective (non-emergency) care obtained from a non-network provider without
prior written approval is covered at a reduced 50% reimbursement rate on the
Basic and Enhanced plans, and is excluded entirely on claims where the member
had access to an equivalent in-network provider within a reasonable
distance, except under the Thiqa and Comprehensive plans which permit
international network access under CG-THQ-002 and CG-COMP-001 respectively.
