# Comprehensive Plan — Coverage Schedule

*Synthetic top-tier plan aimed at executive/expatriate members, styled after
premium UAE private insurance products. Illustrative figures only.*

## Clause CG-COMP-001: Inpatient Hospitalization Coverage

The Comprehensive Plan covers inpatient hospitalization at 100% in any
network facility category (A through D), including international network
facilities for pre-approved treatments not available in the UAE. Private
suite accommodation is included. No per-admission copayment applies.

## Clause CG-COMP-002: Outpatient, Wellness, and Preventive Coverage

All outpatient consultations are covered at 100% with no copayment and no
annual visit cap. The plan additionally covers annual executive health
screenings, preventive cancer screenings (mammography CPT 77067, colonoscopy
CPT 45378) at 100% with no prior authorization required for members over the
recommended screening age.

## Clause CG-COMP-003: Advanced and Elective Procedures

Elective advanced procedures, including cataract surgery (CPT 66984), joint
replacement, and fertility treatment, are covered at 90% subject to prior
authorization. Cosmetic procedures remain excluded under
`exclusions_and_limitations.md` regardless of plan tier.

## Clause CG-COMP-004: Annual Benefit Limit

The Comprehensive Plan carries an annual benefit limit of AED 3,000,000 per
member per policy year, the highest of all four plan tiers offered.
