# Basic Benefit Plan — Coverage Schedule

*Synthetic policy document styled after a DHA-style Basic Benefit Plan for UAE
private-sector employees. All figures are illustrative and for demonstration
purposes only.*

## Clause CG-BASIC-001: Inpatient Hospitalization Coverage

The Basic Plan covers medically necessary inpatient hospitalization in Category
C and D network facilities at 100% of the Standard Fee Schedule, subject to a
per-admission copayment of AED 500. Coverage includes semi-private room
accommodation, nursing care, diagnostic tests ordered during admission, and
surgical procedures classified as essential under the DHA Basic Benefit List.
Admission to Category A or B (premium) facilities is covered only up to the
Category C equivalent rate, with the member responsible for the balance.
Elective admissions require prior authorization at least 3 business days in
advance; emergency admissions must be notified to the insurer within 48 hours.

## Clause CG-BASIC-002: Outpatient Consultation Coverage

Outpatient physician consultations (CPT codes in the 99202-99215 range) are
covered at 80% after a fixed copayment of AED 50 per visit at network general
practitioners, and AED 100 per visit for specialist consultations. Members are
limited to 12 subsidized specialist consultations per policy year under the
Basic tier; consultations beyond this limit are payable at the member's own
expense unless linked to an approved chronic disease management plan (see
`chronic_disease_management.md`).

## Clause CG-BASIC-003: Diagnostic Imaging and Laboratory Coverage

Standard laboratory tests and basic diagnostic imaging (X-ray, ultrasound) are
covered at 80% of the Standard Fee Schedule with no prior authorization
required. Advanced imaging — MRI, CT scan, and nuclear medicine studies — is
covered at 70% and requires prior authorization per
`prior_authorization_requirements.md`. Claims for advanced imaging submitted
without an approved prior-authorization reference number will be denied
under this clause, not escalated, unless the treatment occurred in a
documented emergency.

## Clause CG-BASIC-004: Annual and Lifetime Limits

The Basic Plan carries an annual benefit limit of AED 500,000 per member per
policy year. There is no separate lifetime limit, but benefit categories such
as maternity, dental, and optical are subject to their own sub-limits as
defined in their respective policy documents. Once the annual limit is
exhausted, all subsequent claims in that policy year are automatically
recommended for denial under this clause, pending the member's manual
top-up or renewal.
