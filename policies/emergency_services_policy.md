# Emergency Services Policy

## Clause CG-EMR-001: Definition of a Covered Emergency

A covered emergency is a condition of sudden onset presenting acute symptoms
severe enough that a prudent layperson could reasonably expect that the
absence of immediate medical attention would result in serious jeopardy to
health. Emergency department visits (CPT 99284, 99285) are covered at the
member's standard rate under this definition without prior authorization,
across all plan tiers.

## Clause CG-EMR-002: Diagnostics Performed During an Emergency Encounter

Diagnostic imaging and laboratory tests ordered as part of an emergency
department encounter (e.g., CT head CPT 70450, wound repair CPT 12002) are
exempt from the prior-authorization requirement that would otherwise apply
under `prior_authorization_requirements.md`, provided they are billed under
the same claim as the qualifying emergency department visit code.

## Clause CG-EMR-003: Non-Emergency Use of Emergency Services

Where the diagnosis code and clinical documentation indicate a non-urgent
condition (e.g., routine prescription refill, minor cold symptoms) billed
under an emergency department visit code, the claim should be reimbursed at
the equivalent outpatient GP consultation rate rather than the emergency
rate, and flagged for coding review as a potential emergency-room
misclassification.

## Clause CG-EMR-004: Ambulance and Transport

Emergency ground ambulance transport to the nearest appropriate network
facility is covered at 100% across all plans. Non-emergency patient
transport requires prior authorization.
