# Maternity Coverage Policy

## Clause CG-MAT-001: Antenatal Care

Routine antenatal visits, obstetric ultrasounds (CPT 76805), and fetal
non-stress tests (CPT 59025) are covered at the member's standard outpatient
reimbursement rate for their plan tier, with no prior authorization required
for the first four antenatal visits per trimester.

## Clause CG-MAT-002: Delivery Coverage

Vaginal delivery (CPT 59400) and cesarean delivery (CPT 59510) both require
prior authorization under `prior_authorization_requirements.md`. Basic and
Enhanced plans apply a maternity sub-limit of AED 20,000 and AED 35,000 per
pregnancy respectively; Thiqa and Comprehensive plans have no maternity
sub-limit. A cesarean delivery billed without a documented medical
indication when a vaginal delivery was planned should be flagged by the
Coding Agent for medical-necessity review rather than automatically denied.

## Clause CG-MAT-003: Newborn Coverage

A newborn is automatically covered under the mother's policy for the first
30 days of life for routine care and complications arising from delivery,
after which the newborn must be separately enrolled to continue coverage.

## Clause CG-MAT-004: Waiting Period

Maternity benefits are subject to a 12-month waiting period from the policy
start date for Basic and Enhanced plans. Thiqa and Comprehensive plans waive
this waiting period. Claims for delivery within the waiting period on a
Basic or Enhanced policy should be denied under this clause.
