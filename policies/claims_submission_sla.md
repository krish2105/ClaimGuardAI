# Claims Submission SLA

## Clause CG-SLA-001: Submission Window

Claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the treatment date for
Basic and Enhanced plans, and within 60 calendar days for Thiqa and
Comprehensive plans. Claims submitted after this window are denied under
this clause unless the member can demonstrate good cause for the delay, in
which case the claim should be escalated rather than auto-denied.

## Clause CG-SLA-002: Adjudication Turnaround

Auto-adjudicated claims (auto-approved or auto-denied by the Decision
Router) must be finalized within 24 hours of submission. Claims escalated
to a human adjuster must receive an initial review within 3 business days,
consistent with DHA guidance on timely claims handling.

## Clause CG-SLA-003: Prior Authorization SLA Cross-Reference

Prior-authorization turnaround SLAs are defined separately in Clause
CG-PA-003 and vary by plan tier; this document governs the claims
submission and adjudication window only, not the authorization request
itself.
